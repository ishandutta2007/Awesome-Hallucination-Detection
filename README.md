Top Grant Management (Higher Education) Ecosystem

Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects
Focused on Higher Education Research Administration, Grant Applications, Award Management & Funding Workflows
Last updated: August 2026

This repository tracks notable SaaS/hosted platforms and open-source projects for Grant Management in Higher Education. These tools support research offices, faculty, principal investigators, grant administrators, foundations, and funding organizations across grant discovery, proposal development, application submission, review, award administration, compliance, budgeting, reporting, and closeout.

Examples include Cayuse, InfoEd Global, Huron Research Suite, Streamlyne Research, OpenWater, Submittable, Good Grants, SmartSimple, AmpliFund, and eCivis — spanning research administration platforms, grantmaking systems, application/review platforms, and government funding management.

Open-source emphasis: This section is heavily expanded with open-source and community projects that can serve as building blocks for self-hosted grant-management systems. The open-source ecosystem is considerably smaller than the commercial market, so the list also includes adjacent research-administration, proposal workflow, grant-budgeting, CRM, and funding-allocation projects that can be adapted to higher-education grant workflows. Kuali Coeus is particularly important historically because it was an open-source research-administration platform and is the lineage behind commercial platforms including Kuali Research and Streamlyne.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites or canonical GitHub repositories.

Open-Source GitHub Projects

Gandhi
Open-source online grant-management system built around Node.js, RethinkDB, and Redis. It provides a direct foundation for self-hosted grant application and management workflows.

Smarter Grants Management
U.S. government open-source modernization effort for grants management, intended to support modular workflows spanning funding opportunities, applications, awards, amendments, reporting, and agency-recipient communication.

Grant Budget Management System
Open-source web application focused specifically on grant-budget management, including grant records, role-based permissions, multi-year budgets, salary/fringe/indirect calculations, and spreadsheet exports.

GPMS-NGAC
Grant Proposal Management System for academic institutions, providing a web-based workflow for automating proposal-submission approval processes.

Swiss Polar Institute Project Application
Open-source project for tracking research projects and proposals from application through completion, making it relevant as a research-grant workflow building block.

OpenBcon
Open-source funding and business-consultant workspace for discovering funding programs, managing organizations and applications, generating funding packages, and organizing resources and reports. It uses an AGPL community edition with commercial licensing for certain deployments.

GrantFlow.ai
Open-source-oriented project for creating grant applications with ML/AI capabilities. Its current licensing is dual-licensed under BSL 1.1 and Apache 2.0 with a future conversion to Apache 2.0.

CiviCRM + CiviGrant
Open-source CRM platform with grant-related functionality that can be adapted for nonprofit, philanthropic, and funding workflows. CiviCRM is particularly useful when grant management needs to be integrated with constituents, organizations, communications, and fundraising.

OJS — Open Journal Systems
Open-source scholarly publishing platform that can be adapted for structured submission and peer-review workflows resembling grant calls and proposal-review processes.

OpenReview
Open-source platform for structured submission, review, discussion, and decision workflows. Although primarily designed for scholarly peer review, its architecture can be adapted to competitive grant and research-proposal evaluation.

Janeway
Open-source scholarly publishing platform with submission and peer-review workflows that can serve as a reusable foundation for structured proposal intake and review.

ProjeQtOr
Open-source project-management platform that can be configured to track funded projects, milestones, deliverables, budgets, tasks, risks, and reporting obligations.

ERPNext
Open-source ERP that can provide financial, project, accounting, HR, and organizational building blocks for a custom post-award grant-management system.

Odoo Community
Open-source ERP framework that can be customized for grant budgets, project accounting, approvals, contacts, procurement, reporting, and funded-project management.

Additional Strong Open-Source Options

Kuali Coeus — Historically important open-source research-administration software developed by the higher-education community and the predecessor/lineage for several commercial research-administration offerings.

CiviCRM — Open-source CRM foundation for organizations that need grant management integrated with constituents, donors, partners, and communications.

OpenReview — Submission, reviewer assignment, peer review, discussion, and decision infrastructure that can be adapted to grant competitions.

OJS — Mature open-source submission and review infrastructure suitable for adaptation to proposal workflows.

Janeway — Open scholarly workflow platform with submission, editorial, review, and publication pipelines.

ProjeQtOr — Project-management foundation for post-award project tracking, milestones, budgets, and deliverables.

ERPNext — ERP foundation for finance, accounting, projects, procurement, and post-award administration.

Odoo — Flexible ERP framework for implementing custom grant, project, approval, and financial workflows.

Grant-management GitHub ecosystem — A useful discovery point for smaller grant-management applications, prototypes, grant-budget tools, and research projects. GitHub currently lists dozens of repositories under the grants-management topic.

Important open-source gap: Unlike areas such as CRM, ERP, workflow orchestration, and research publishing, there are relatively few mature open-source products that provide a complete Cayuse/InfoEd/Huron-style higher-education research administration suite. Current open-source projects tend to focus on individual pieces such as grant applications, proposal workflows, budgeting, funding discovery, or general CRM/project management.

Frameworks for building a custom higher-education grant platform: Combine CiviCRM for organizations and relationships, Gandhi/OpenBcon for grant workflows, OpenReview/OJS/Janeway for submission and review, ERPNext/Odoo for financial and project administration, and ProjeQtOr for funded-project tracking. A university can then connect these components through APIs and workflow automation to create a modular alternative to a traditional monolithic research-administration suite.

Disclaimer

The higher-education grant-management market overlaps heavily with research administration, sponsored programs, grantmaking, nonprofit grant management, and government grants management.
